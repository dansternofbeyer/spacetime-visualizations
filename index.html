Spacetime Visualizations

Interactive 3D views of space and time, driven by real physics. Each page lists its equations, sources and simplifications in a Physics notes panel. Add ?selftest to a page's address to see its physics checks.

Prototype

CfA2 Galaxy Map

About 18,000 real galaxies around the Milky Way, placed in 3D by their redshift. Compare the ΛCDM and Hubble-law distances, change H₀, and see the 1986 "slice of the universe".
Planned

Gravitational Redshift

Light climbing out of a mass's gravity well (Schwarzschild metric, seen from far away), with a warped grid and an embedding-diagram view.

The Observable Universe

Our past light cone as a sphere: today at the centre, recombination (the cosmic microwave background) at the edge.
