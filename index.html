Spacetime Visualizations

Interactive 3D views of space and time, driven by real physics. Each page lists its equations, sources and simplifications in a Physics notes panel. Add ?selftest to a page's address to see its physics checks.

Prototype

Galaxy Redshift Map

About 158,000 real galaxies around the Milky Way from the CfA2 (north), 6dFGS (south) and 2MRS (whole sky) surveys, placed in 3D by their redshift. Compare the ΛCDM and Hubble-law distances, change H₀, and see the 1986 "slice of the universe".

 Prototype

Gravitational Redshift

Light climbing out of a gravity well (Schwarzschild metric, seen from far away). Change the mass, move the emitter and receiver, slow the light down, and see space drawn as a warped cube grid or as Flamm's paraboloid.

 Prototype

Two Body Problem

Light passing between two stars in an exact static two-body solution (Weyl / Israel–Khan). It reddens climbing out of one star's gravity and blues falling into the other's. Compare clocks along the path and as seen by observers you place.

The Observable Universe

Our past light cone as a sphere: today at the centre, recombination (the cosmic microwave background) at the edge.
